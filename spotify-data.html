Request Extended Streaming History, then explore it here

Parse Spotify’s Extended Streaming History JSON in your browser — sample files from data/ or your own upload. Filter, chart, and export structured Excel. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

How to Request Your Data

Use Spotify’s official Download your data tool. For full listening analysis, request Extended streaming history (lifetime play log with reason codes, skip flags, platform, and more).

  1. Open Account Privacy

    Sign in at spotify.com/account/privacy in a web browser (not the mobile app).

  2. Find “Download your data”

    Request Extended streaming history for the richest play log. Account data includes a shorter recent streaming history with fewer fields.

  3. Request and confirm

    Submit the request, then confirm via the email Spotify sends. Without confirmation, the export may not start.

  4. Wait for the download link

    Extended history can take up to about 30 days. Download promptly when the email arrives — links expire.

  5. Unzip and load JSON here

    Look for files named like Streaming_History_Audio_*.json and Streaming_History_Video_*.json. Upload them below (or place samples in data/ and use Load sample).

Official references: Data rights and privacy · Understanding your data

What’s in Extended Streaming History

Each JSON file is an array of stream objects. Music, podcasts, and audiobooks share the same shape; video streams use the same fields in Video files. IP addresses may be present in the raw JSON — this app never charts them and omits them from Excel export.

Identity of the play

ts (UTC end time), track or episode name, artist/show, album, Spotify URI, ms_played, platform, country.

How it started / ended

reason_start / reason_end (e.g. trackdone, fwdbtn, endplay), plus shuffle, skipped, offline, and incognito flags.

Content types

Music via track metadata; podcasts via episode fields; audiobooks via audiobook fields. Audio vs video is inferred from the filename.

Load Your Files

Upload one or more Extended Streaming History .json files. Processing stays on this device. Sample files in the gitignored data/ folder can be loaded when you serve the site locally (e.g. python -m http.server 8000).
