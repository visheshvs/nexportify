Feature 01

Spotify Playlist Metadata

Turn a playlist into a dataset: track details, artists, genres, record labels, release dates and popularity, plus audio features such as danceability, energy, valence and tempo when they're available. Pick the mode that fits.

Quick start

Simple Analysis

Sign in and analyze any of your playlists right away: genres, artists, labels, release years and popularity. No export needed.

  1. Sign in with Spotify
  2. Browse your playlists below
  3. Click Analyze on a playlist, or Export to save its CSV

You're signed in. Your playlists are listed below.

Full data

Advanced Analysis

Every audio feature, including mood quadrant, tempo, key and energy, from a CSV exported with exportify.net.

  1. Go to exportify.net and sign in
  2. Export your playlist as CSV
  3. Upload the CSV here

Feature 02

Listening Data Exploration

A playlist shows what you saved. Your streaming history shows what you actually played. Load the Extended Streaming History from Spotify's official data download to explore years of plays, with all processing done in your browser.

Habits & rhythm

Listening time over days, weeks and years, a weekday × hour listening clock, streaks and session lengths.

Taste & discovery

Top artists and tracks, how concentrated your taste is, and how many new artists you discover each month.

Attention & obsessions

Skip and completion rates, on-repeat streaks, comeback tracks, plus platforms and countries over time.

  1. Request Extended streaming history at spotify.com/account/privacy
  2. Wait for Spotify's email (can take up to ~30 days)
  3. Upload the Streaming_History_*.json files
